Zeeman Weak-Field Picture: Double Precession

Spin-orbit coupling acts like a strong internal torque. The external magnetic field acts like a weaker external torque. Vectors L and S rapidly precess around J, while J slowly precesses around the external field B.
weak field: B_out << B_in
B_out: external magnetic field
J = L + S: total angular momentum
L: orbital angular momentum, fast around J
S: spin angular momentum, fast around J

Layer 1: internal precession
Spin-orbit coupling locks L and S into the total angular momentum J. They are not static; they rapidly precess around J.
omega_in proportional to B_in
Layer 2: external precession
The external field weakly perturbs the whole atomic magnetic moment, so J slowly precesses around B_out. In this limit, j and m_j remain good quantum numbers.
omega_out proportional to B_out
Why the weak-field formula works
When B_out is much smaller than B_in, the external field cannot break the L-S coupling. It only sees the averaged total magnetic moment mu_J.
Delta E = g_j mu_B m_j B
